COMMON REQUIREMENTS FOR ALL BLENDER ASSETS

Create original game-ready 3D environment assets for Blender intended for export to Godot 4 as GLB.

Visual style: premium stylized low-poly 3D, simplified but believable geometry, strong silhouettes, realistic scale, detailed PBR materials, subtle bevels, high-quality normals, moderate surface wear, cinematic retro-futuristic post-apocalyptic design.

Do not copy specific copyrighted locations, logos, symbols, brands or recognizable game assets.

Technical requirements:
- real-world scale;
- modular construction wherever possible;
- clean topology;
- sensible UV mapping;
- separate materials;
- optimized geometry for real-time rendering;
- clean object hierarchy;
- correct origins and pivots;
- no unnecessary hidden geometry;
- important moving parts as separate objects;
- reusable modules align to a 1-meter grid;
- architecture easy to assemble in Godot;
- no characters;
- no baked cinematic lighting in textures;
- environment looks good from first-person camera height.


# Rocks and cliffs

Create a wasteland rock and cliff asset pack for procedural scattering.

Generate approximately 12 distinct assets:
- 5 medium rocks;
- 3 large rocks;
- 2 long eroded rock formations;
- 1 vertical cliff piece;
- 1 broken rocky arch or overhang.

Use simplified stylized geometry with believable erosion. Every asset should have a strong silhouette from several directions and look natural when duplicated, rotated and scaled randomly.
